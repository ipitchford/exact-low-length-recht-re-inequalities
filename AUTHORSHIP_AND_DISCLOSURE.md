# Authorship and AI-use disclosure

## Journal-submission authorship decision

The inherited manuscripts used model-authorship language and identified the
person who supplied research direction. Neither attribution is carried into
the anonymous paper or public replication archive. Research direction alone
is not presumed to satisfy a journal's authorship criteria.

Before any journal submission, an accountable human author must:

- inspect and approve the full mathematical claims and proof exposition;
- accept responsibility for the accuracy and integrity of the work;
- approve the release of the certificate code and data;
- provide affiliation, contact details, ORCID, funding, conflicts, and CRediT
  roles; and
- replace or approve the provisional anonymous author and citation records.

## Recommended publication roles

Record human contributions using the target venue's CRediT vocabulary. Useful
categories for explicit confirmation are conceptualization, methodology,
software, validation, formal analysis, investigation, writing—original draft,
writing—review and editing, supervision, and project administration.
