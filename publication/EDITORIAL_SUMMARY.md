# Editorial summary

## Proposed title

**Exact Low-Length Recht--Ré Inequalities: Complete status through five
factors and six-factor balanced families**

## Contributions

1. A two-sided four-factor theorem for every \(n\ge4\).
2. A five-factor upper theorem for every \(n\ge5\), a lower theorem for every
   \(n\ge6\), and an exact rational failure at \(n=5\), giving a sharp lower
   restoration threshold.
3. A general balanced-seed continuation theorem separating coefficient
   identity from positivity.
4. New six-factor infinite families: upper on \(7\mathbb N\), lower on
   \(8\mathbb N\), and two-sided on \(56\mathbb N\).
5. An exact one-epoch quadratic bias--mean-square reversal on De Sa's witness.

## Why one paper

The four- and five-factor results share one free-localizer, symmetry,
continuation, and positivity architecture. The six-factor result is a direct
application of the general balanced theorem using full seed PSD rather than
uniform blocks. The quadratic proposition explains the stochastic-
optimization meaning and limitation of the first-moment matrix inequality.

## Evidence

The anonymous paper is accompanied by exact rational data, two arithmetic
replays and derivations for \(m=4,5\), complementary identity/FLINT programs
for \(m=6\), a structurally separate semantic checker, exhaustive quadratic
path enumeration, bidirectional SHA-256 manifests, and mutation controls
under normal and optimized Python.

## Review emphasis

Arithmetic replay is routine. Expert review should focus on the semantic
reduction, representation coverage, balanced-coloring scaling, six-factor
orbit reconstruction, and the scope of the optimization interpretation.
