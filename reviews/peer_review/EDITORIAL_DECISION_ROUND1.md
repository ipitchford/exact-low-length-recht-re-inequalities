# Editorial decision after internal round 1

> **Historical status note (30 July 2026).** This decision records the state
> at the end of internal round 1. Its pre-publication, license, and DOI
> conclusions are superseded by the scoped CC0-1.0 candidate release and exact
> version DOI documented in `STATUS.md`. The mathematical review targets and
> unresolved journal-authorship boundary remain current.

**Decision:** major revision completed; retain as an anonymous
pre-publication candidate and seek external expert review.

The initial editor, domain, methodology, optimization-perspective, clean-room,
and devil's-advocate reports found no counterexample to the m4/m5 theorem
chain but identified semantic-reduction, traceability, literature, and
packaging gaps. Those reports describe the earlier manuscript and are
preserved unchanged as an audit trail.

## Resolved major findings

| Round-1 concern | Resolution |
|---|---|
| Shared orbit/schema logic could verify the wrong problem | Added a concrete-word semantic checker using literal permutation actions, cyclic representation spans, finite-difference degree checks, and full Gram LDL tests |
| Acceptance depended on removable Python assertions | Replaced load-bearing assertions; added AST safety scan and normal/`-O` mutation controls |
| General continuation mechanism was implicit | Added and proved the balanced-seed continuation theorem, explicitly separating rational identity from positivity |
| Literature framing was narrow | Expanded to 16 primary references and clarified adjacent AGM, reshuffling, single-shuffle, and second-moment statements |

| Exploratory m6 material lacked theorem evidence | Reconstructed all 2,312 identities and exact full seed PSD; an independent audit supports upper \(7\mid n\), lower \(8\mid n\), and two-sided \(56\mid n\) only |
| Manifests and traceability were stale | Added three complete child manifests, one package manifest, exact dependency pins, receipts, and bidirectional validation |
| Package contained multiple named papers | Removed legacy manuscripts/source copies and retained one anonymous paper |

## Findings intentionally not closed

- The historical numerical discovery computations remain unreproduced.
- The all-\(n\) six-factor representation bases remain missing.
- The endpoint m6 dual-looking artifacts have no theorem implication.
- No formal proof assistant or genuinely independent external implementation
  has verified the complete paper.
- Accountable journal authorship and venue declarations remain human
  submission decisions. The public candidate's scoped CC0-1.0 dedication and
  exact version DOI were resolved after this round.

## Editorial recommendation

The revised work is substantially stronger than the round-1 manuscript and
is suitable for circulation as an anonymous exact-computation candidate.
External referees should prioritize the semantic free-polynomial reduction,
balanced-coloring scaling, representation completeness, and independent
reconstruction of the six-factor seed matrices.
